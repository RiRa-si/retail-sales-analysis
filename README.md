# Retail Sales Analysis Using Pandas

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a small synthetic retail sales dataset using Python and Pandas. The goal is to understand product performance, regional profitability, monthly sales trends, and profit margins.

## Objectives

- Calculate revenue and profit.
- Compare product and regional performance.
- Analyze monthly revenue trends.
- Calculate product and regional profit margins.
- Communicate findings through visualizations.
- Develop evidence-based business recommendations.

## Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Key Findings

- Total revenue: ₹710,000
- Total profit: ₹160,000
- Overall profit margin: 22.54%
- Laptops generated the highest product revenue and total profit.
- Tablets had the highest product profit margin at 30.83%.
- North generated the highest regional revenue and profit margin.
- April 2026 had the highest monthly revenue at ₹230,000.
- East had the lowest regional profit margin at approximately 20.56%.

## Business Recommendations

1. Investigate pricing, costs, and discounts in the East region.
2. Evaluate opportunities to grow tablet sales while monitoring demand and costs.
3. Investigate the factors behind April's high revenue before assuming the pattern will repeat.

## Limitations

The dataset contains only 10 synthetic orders created for learning purposes. The findings are illustrative and should not be generalized to a real retail business. A larger, representative dataset would be needed to validate these patterns.
